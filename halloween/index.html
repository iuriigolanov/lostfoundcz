Halloween FAQ

When is Lost Halloween at Lost & Found?
Friday 30 and Saturday 31 October 2026. Doors Friday from 20:00, Saturday from 21:00, party till 3 AM. Free entry — come in costume.
Is there an entry fee?
No — Lost Halloween is free. Just come in costume and order from the Halloween menu (cocktails from 175 Kč) or share a hookah (535 Kč).
What's on the Halloween menu?
Spooky signature cocktails like Zombie, Vampire Kiss, Blood Moon and Candy Killer, plus flaming shots such as Black Cat and Black Widow. Our full bar menu is available too.
Should I book a table for Halloween?
Yes, Friday 30 and Saturday 31 October get busy. Book a table on WhatsApp or call +420 724 188 537.
What about The Fright Crawl?
Lost & Found is also a stop on The Fright Crawl bar crawl (Thu 29 – Sat 31 October). That's a separate ticketed route from €15 on frightcrawl.com — not needed to join our free Lost Halloween.
Can I host a private Halloween party?
Yes. Our basement lounge fits up to 50 guests for team and birthday Halloween parties — see private events on the home page.
Where is Lost & Found?
Žitná 1764/11, Nové Město, Prague 1 — a few minutes' walk from Wenceslas Square.
