Who we are

The best-kept secret on Žitná street

Lost & Found is a cocktail bar and hookah lounge minutes from Wenceslas Square in Prague's Nové Město. Signature craft cocktails, premium hookah, board games, darts and our boxing machine — the kind of late-night room you stumble into once and keep coming back to.

We also host private events in Prague: birthdays, company parties and music nights, with catering and a bespoke cocktail menu. Rated 4.9★ across 1,090+ Google reviews — one of the highest-rated cocktail bars in Prague and a top-rated nightlife spot in Nové Město.

Lost & Found is a proud LGBT-friendly bar and an official partner of Prague Pride. Everyone is welcome here, all year round. 💜

Why Prague chooses Lost & Found

Our bartenders create exclusive signature cocktails that go beyond the classics. Drinks like Ferrari, Lamborghini, and Long Island are carefully crafted with premium spirits. Every guest is welcomed with genuine hospitality — our bartenders will gladly advise, recommend the perfect drink, or create a custom cocktail just for you.

🎡 Premium Hookah & Entertainment

Beyond cocktails, we offer premium hookah lounge with carefully selected blends (Tropical Mix, Berry Madness, Citrus Fresh) at 495 CZK. Enjoy free board games, darts, table football, and boxing machine. We host events and have dedicated spaces for private gatherings.

🍺 Craft Beer, Full Bar & Late Night

Curated selection of craft beers, premium spirits, wines, and alcohol-free options. We accept card and cash payments — full flexibility. Located on Žitná in Nové Město's center, we're open until 1:00 AM Wed–Thu and 3:00 AM Fri–Sat. The relaxed, welcoming atmosphere has no pretense — just genuine neon vibes.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Everything people usually message us about before they drop by.

Do you take reservations at Lost & Found Bar Prague?

Yes. You can reserve a table online in a few seconds, and walk-ins are always welcome too. Weekends fill up fast, so booking ahead is a good idea if you want a specific spot. For bigger groups or anything special, just get in touch and we will sort it out.

What time does Lost & Found Bar Prague close?

Sunday, Monday and Tuesday we are open 18:00–00:00. Wednesday and Thursday 17:00–01:00. Friday and Saturday we go late: 17:00–03:00. Need us at a different time — daytime, morning, or anything outside our usual hours? Contact us and we will make it happen.

Do you have hookah or shisha?

Yes. Lost & Found is a full hookah lounge as well as a cocktail bar. We serve premium shisha alongside our signature craft cocktails, and you can enjoy both in the same seating. See the hookah menu.

Where is Lost & Found Bar Prague located?

We are at Žitná 1764/11, Nové Město, Praha 1, in the heart of Prague — just minutes on foot from Wenceslas Square, with I. P. Pavlova and Karlovo náměstí metro stations both a short walk away.

Is there anything to do besides drinking?

Plenty. We have arcade and bar games including a boxing machine, foosball and darts, plus a shelf of board games. It is a bar built for staying a while, not just one round. We also host private events, parties and special occasions, and can put together custom catering on request — tell us what you have in mind.

Can I book Lost & Found for a private event or party?

Yes. We host birthdays, corporate nights, team socials and full bar buyouts. We have three spaces — a 7-seat bar counter, a main floor for up to 25 with mobile tables, and a basement lounge for up to 50 with its own bar, darts and foosball — so we can take anything from a small group to around 80 guests. See the spaces or message us with your dates and we will build the night around you.

Do you do food and catering for events?

We do. We have a full kitchen on site, so we cook in-house, work with our suppliers, or combine both depending on what your event needs — from canapés to a full spread. Pricing depends on the day, group size, spaces and menu, so send us the details and we will come back with a quote.
