Menu FAQ

How much is a cocktail at Lost & Found?
Cocktails cost 165–290 Kč. Classics like Mojito, Negroni or Margarita are 175–220 Kč, signature cocktails 185–290 Kč, alcohol-free cocktails 145–180 Kč.
What are the signature cocktails?
Ferrari, Crimson Kiss, Lamborghini, Shrek, Wolf Blood, Skittles and more — 18 house creations you won't find anywhere else in Prague.
How much is a hookah?
Every hookah costs 535 Kč, whichever blend you choose.
Do you serve food?
Yes — pizza from 255 Kč, quesadillas from 180 Kč, nachos, nuts and chips.
Do you have alcohol-free drinks?
Yes: 0.0% cocktails such as Virgin Colada, alcohol-free Gin Tonic and Mojito, plus homemade lemonades from 85 Kč.
Can I pay by card?
Yes, we accept card and cash.
