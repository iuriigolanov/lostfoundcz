Why smoke shisha with us

Comfortable lounge seating, a full cocktail bar next to you and a relaxed crowd of locals and travellers. Pair your hookah with a signature cocktail like Ferrari or Crimson Kiss, a classic Mojito or an alcohol-free Virgin Colada — see the full menu.

Coming with friends? Grab a board game, play darts or table football — it's free. For birthdays and company parties there is a private basement lounge for up to 50 guests (private events).

FAQ

How much is a hookah at Lost & Found?
Every hookah costs 535 Kč, whichever blend you choose — including the Hookah of the Day mixed by our hookah master.
Which hookah flavours do you have?
Tropical Mix, Berry Madness, Citrus Fresh, Sweet Dream and Exotic Ice, plus a custom Hookah of the Day. Ask the staff for today's options.
Where is the hookah lounge?
Žitná 1764/11, Nové Město, Prague 1 — a few minutes' walk from Wenceslas Square.
When are you open?
Sunday–Tuesday 18:00–00:00, Wednesday–Thursday 17:00–01:00, Friday–Saturday 17:00–03:00.
Do I need a reservation?
Walk-ins are welcome. For groups and Friday–Saturday nights we recommend booking a table via WhatsApp or by phone: +420 724 188 537.
Can I order cocktails with my hookah?
Yes — the full bar is available: 60 cocktails, shots, craft beer and alcohol-free drinks, plus pizza and snacks.
